# Specs

Build specifications and decision records for audit skills.

A spec lands here when the design work is finished and the implementation is not.
Once a plugin exists under `plugins/`, its spec stays as the record of what was
decided and why — the decision logs are the part that does not survive in code.

|---|---|---|
| `place-audit-build-spec-v1.0.md` | `place-audit` | Implemented; retained as the v1 decision record, with v2 workflow corrections noted inline |
